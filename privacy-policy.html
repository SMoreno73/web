Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Instituto Tecnológico Metropolitano (ITM) (“ITM,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when students use the Course AI Tutor through WhatsApp. The service is intended to provide AI-assisted answers to course questions using relevant educational context made available to it.

Information the service may process

To operate the service, ITM may process:

  • WhatsApp account details, such as a phone number and profile name, and message delivery identifiers.
  • Messages, questions, and files or other content a student chooses to send.
  • Course-related context needed to answer a question, such as course names, learning materials, or relevant enrollment information.
  • Technical and operational records, such as timestamps, delivery status, error logs, and service diagnostics.

How information is used

Information is used to receive and respond to course-related questions, provide relevant educational context to the tutor, deliver WhatsApp messages, maintain and troubleshoot the service, protect it from misuse, and meet institutional obligations. The tutor provides educational support and does not make academic decisions.

How information is handled

Messages are transmitted through the WhatsApp Cloud API and processed by the tutor backend, Moodle or relevant course context made available to the tutor, hosting services, and an AI provider configured by ITM. These systems use the information to generate and deliver course-related responses and operate the service.

ITM does not use the information for purposes unrelated to operating, securing, troubleshooting, and supporting the educational service, except as required to meet institutional obligations.

Retention

Messages and operational records are retained only for as long as necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service and meet institutional obligations. Retention and deletion are handled according to ITM’s institutional procedures; no fixed retention period is stated here.

Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by WhatsApp, Moodle, hosting services, and the AI provider configured by ITM, as needed to provide messaging, course context, hosting, and AI-generated responses. Authorized ITM personnel may access information when needed to operate or support the service.

Each provider processes information under its applicable terms and privacy practices. ITM should review this policy when providers or processing arrangements change.

Security

Public pages and API traffic use HTTPS. Incoming WhatsApp webhooks are validated using Meta’s signature mechanism. Access tokens and other secrets are kept in restricted environment configuration rather than source files, and operational access is controlled. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and privacy requests

Students may stop using the tutor by no longer messaging it. To ask about, access, correct, or request deletion of information, or to raise a privacy concern, email montoyamorenojhonsebastian@gmail.com. Do not include passwords or unnecessary sensitive information in the request. ITM will review and handle requests according to its institutional procedures.

Data deletion instructions

To request deletion of information associated with your use of the tutor, email montoyamorenojhonsebastian@gmail.com with the subject Data deletion request. Include your WhatsApp number and enough information for ITM to identify the request. ITM will review and process it according to its institutional procedures. No fixed processing deadline is promised.

Children and younger students

The service is intended for students enrolled in relevant courses. Questions about a younger student’s use may be directed to montoyamorenojhonsebastian@gmail.com. ITM should review the service’s suitability and any applicable institutional procedures before making it available to younger students.

Updates to this policy

We may update this policy when the service or its information practices change. The current version will be published at this URL, with its effective date shown above. The organization should provide any additional notice required by its procedures.
